# Wine-quality report — review draft, version 1

## Aim

Describe the observed relationships of alcohol and volatile acidity with wine
quality. The original report remains preserved.

## Data and method

Coding validated the dataset and used a quality score of 7 or above for the
high-quality group. The analysis is descriptive; no intervention was performed.

## Results and interpretation

The analysis grouped wines with quality score 7 or above as high quality. Alcohol and volatile acidity were associated with quality in the checked descriptive output. **Higher alcohol causes higher wine quality.** The report has not been published.
